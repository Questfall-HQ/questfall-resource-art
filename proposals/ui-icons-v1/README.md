# UI icon candidates v1

`submissions.png` is a transparent PNG concept generated with the built-in
image generation tool for the Submissions marker. It follows the current inbox
or tray silhouette used in Questfall, with one pale paper sheet entering a
muted fuchsia tray. The candidate is previewed at 12–64 px and is not active.

Generation prompt: one broad, readable inbox and incoming sheet silhouette for
a dark RPG interface, soft fuchsia and lilac with pale paper, subtle volume,
transparent square canvas, no text, checkmark, arrow, badge, background, or
extra documents. Keep it legible at 16 px.

Refinement prompt: preserve the composition and paper/tray separation while
reducing bright rim and glossy highlights to calm matte shading; keep enough
contrast on a near-black surface. The refined result is the saved candidate.

Three additional concepts explore different meanings of a submission while
keeping the same quiet, transparent game-item style. Each was generated with
the built-in image generation tool for legibility at 20 px:

- `submissions-form.png`: a completed ivory answer form with two bold response
  lines and one large fuchsia check. Prompt: "one sturdy nearly square answer
  form, two broad lines and an integrated check, no second document or tray."
- `submissions-proof.png`: a landscape photo frame with simplified scenery and
  one prominent fuchsia check. Prompt: "photo proof as the primary rectangular
  silhouette, no sheet, tray, or camera body."
- `submissions-send.png`: a folded paper plane with a short fuchsia motion
  trail. Prompt: "the act of sending a quest result; one broad paper-plane
  silhouette, no document card, tray, envelope, or checkmark."

Shared generation constraints: genuinely transparent canvas, centered isolated
object, calm sculpted 3D materials, muted lavender/fuchsia with ivory and cool
gray, broad readable shapes, no text, glow, background, or tiny detail.
